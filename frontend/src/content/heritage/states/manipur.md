## History & Origin

Manipur possesses one of India's most refined handloom cultures, historically sustained almost entirely by skilled women artisans weaving inside household *iyong* sheds across the Imphal Valley.

**Wangkhei Phee** originated as gossamer white royal court muslin reserved for noble ceremonies, while **Moirang Phee** gained renown around Loktak Lake for its razor-sharp black, wine, or gold temple borders (**Moirangphi**, said to mirror thin eyelashes of the Meitei patron deity Thangjing). Both lineages flow gracefully today into full Manipuri handloom sarees.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Wangkhei and Kongba** in Imphal East preserve featherlight *Wangkhei Phee* muslins, while **Moirang and Bishnupur** keep *Moirang Phee*'s stepped geometric spires thriving alongside striped *Phanek* borders rich in *Khoi Mayek* hook symbolism.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Pure cotton-and-silk *Wangkhei* drapes feel weightlessly porous—catching gentle movement effortlessly in humid summer air while letting fine contrasting *buttis* glow delicately across soft cream, blush, or pistachio pastel fields.

Meanwhile, **Moirang Phee** anchors crisp translucent bodies with graphic, unmistakable architectural temple spikes along the hem.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

Woven traditionally on hand-operated throw-shuttle frame looms without punched cards, each graduated **Moirangphi** tooth is counted and laid in with extra-weft shuttles by eye so the jagged peaks step steadily inward toward the saree center without puckering the sheer ground.

## Cultural Significance

Treasured across Lai Haraoba celebrations, Ras Lila classical traditions, and serene wedding trousseaus, Manipur's crisp temple weaves carry disciplined grace and timeless visual calm.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Dry-clean silk-cotton Moirang Phee and heavily motifed Wangkhei Phee drapes; pure unembellished fine cottons may be soaked very briefly in cold gentle cleanser without scrubbing.
- Never wring delicate gossamer muslin—roll gently inside a dry white cotton towel and dry flat in cool shade.
- Press gently from the reverse while slightly damp so tapered temple borders sit crisp and flat.
- Store wrapped inside clean cotton covers away from bright light.

## History & Origin

Worn historically by Goa's hardworking **Kunbi** and **Gawda** rural women—traditionally knotted cleanly over one shoulder as a breezy knee-to-ankle **kapod**—the checkered Kunbi cloth stood for centuries as Goa's signature native handloom identity.

Though commercial mill cloth nearly displaced Goa's weaving looms in the late 20th century, dedicated Goan revivalists and textile scholars resurrected the classic check formula on cooperative handlooms—translating the iconic pattern into effortless full six-yard sarees prized today.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

Historic **Bardez, Salcete, and Ponda** memories shape modern Goan handloom revivals, keeping faith with classic Konkan palettes of vibrant *madder-red*, sunlit terracotta, forest green, and ivory white punctuated by crisp *dobby* borders.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Pure cotton Kunbi sarees weave airy durability directly into high-twist yarn, absorbing coastal humidity cleanly while keeping crisp, structured pleats without stiff starching.

Soft to the touch after the very first wash, they drape smoothly in warm tropical mornings and festive folk gatherings alike.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

Alternating dyed crimson and natural off-white yarns in both warp and weft create classic Konkan *madras*-like micro-checks across the ground, framed neatly with tiny geometric *dobby* triangles and bold contrasting stripes along the border and pallu.

## Cultural Significance

Seen vividly during *Shigmo*, *Dhalo*, and Konkan cultural festivals—and worn warmly across contemporary coastal brunches and heritage events—the Kunbi saree embodies Konkan resilience, coastal warmth, and proud regional craft revival.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Hand-wash cotton Kunbi check sarees gently in cold water with mild soap—wash separately on the first couple washes so rich crimson dye settles cleanly.
- Line-dry smoothly in coastal or shaded breeze without direct harsh afternoon glare.
- Press with medium iron warmth while lightly damp to sharpen crisp checks.
- Store folded loosely inside an airy cupboard.

## History & Origin

Across **Almora, Champawat, and Nainital** in Kumaon—and throughout the Garhwal hills—textiles mirror ceremonial life with joyful symbolic economy. For centuries, Kumaoni brides and married women draped the beloved **Rangwali Pichora** (saffron-yellow ground dotted vividly with crimson symbols stamped using coins wrapped in cotton dipped in vermilion and turmeric).

Alongside Pichora's saffron-and-crimson ritual palette, Uttarakhand's hill districts cultivate soft **Oak Tussar**, Himalayan mulberry, and warm merino-silk winter weaves crafted around **Bhimtal, Almora, and Chamoli**.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Almora and Bhimtal** weave serene winter tussar and tweed-textured silk-wool drapes tinted gently with walnut husk, madder root, and wild marigold, while Kumaon's traditional **Rangwali** craft brings vibrant saffron-red dotted sarees and bridal veils to every mountain ceremony.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Oak Tussar woven across Uttarakhand carries a slightly smoother, softer handle than dense forest varieties—pairing with fine wool warps to produce supple, breathable warmth.

Rangwali Pichora sarees, traditionally crafted on sheer *mulmul*, crisp *georgette*, or pure *silk*, ripple joyfully with fiery saffron energy brightened by crimson circles.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

In **Pichora** craft, artisans tint the base cloth a luminous golden saffron with turmeric or mineral dye before hand-stamping crisp rows of deep *sindoor* circles (*bindis*) flanking central *swastika*, sun, bell, and conch medallions inspired by **Aipan** threshold art.

Meanwhile, Bhimtal's handloom ateliers weave fine diamond twills and quiet temple borders across earthy mountain hues.

## Cultural Significance

Essential during Namkaran, Upanayan, Harela, and Kumaoni wedding processions, Uttarakhand's luminous saffron-crimson dots and warm hill silks symbolize sunlit blessing and serene mountain heritage.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Dry-clean Pichora silk/georgette drapes and Oak Tussar silk-wool sarees gently; traditional natural turmeric and sindoor tones bleed if washed carelessly in warm water.
- Store shaded inside unbleached cotton cloth away from bright sunlight to keep saffron radiance vivid.
- Steam gently from behind rather than pressing hot metal plates over stamped borders.
- Keep winter wool-silks neatly folded flat during summer.

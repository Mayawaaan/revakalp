## History & Origin

Jharkhand's dense Chotanagpur Sal and Asan forests produce the lion's share of India's organic *Antheraea mylitta* wild tussar cocoons. For tribal farming families across Singhbhum and Santhal Pargana, silk cocoon rearing has sustained seasonal forest harmony for generations.

Along with spinning sturdy honey-toned silk, local handloom weavers across **Bhagaiya, Godda, and Kuchai** crafted earthy checked sarees and traditional Santhal *Panchi-Parhan* wraps distinguished by unmistakable horizontal border bands.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Kuchai and Kharsawan** in Seraikela Kharsawan district lead organic forest tussar reeling and spinning, while **Bhagaiya and Godda** house brisk pit-loom clusters weaving tussar-silk, matka, and cotton blends accented with temple geometry and Sohrai-inspired folk block prints.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Kuchai tussar exhibits slightly finer sheen and crisp structural resilience than coarse spun silk—draping with a poised sculptural silhouette suited to daytime formalwear and warm-climate celebrations.

Cotton *Santhali* bordered drapes offer supple weightlessness and vivid rhythmic contrast between dark charcoal or indigo fields and blazing vermilion-ochre borders.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

Women spinners gently soften boiled cocoons before rolling yarns by thigh (*ghicha*) or motorized hand-reeling wheels (*buniyaad*), preserving tiny slubs that catch sunlit highlights across plain and twill ground weaves.

Border weavers pair natural undyed tussar centers with rich *dobby* diamond strips, temple teeth, or wax-batik and Sohrai mural brushstrokes.

## Cultural Significance

From Karam and Sohrai harvest festivals across Jharkhand's plateau to poised urban collections nationwide, Kuchai Tussar connects forest ecological craft smoothly to contemporary ceremonial wardrobes.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Dry-clean pure Kuchai Tussar sarees gently to preserve natural gum sheen and structural pleats.
- Hand-wash dark cotton-bordered Santhali sarees separately in cold water using mild soap.
- Air dry flat away from harsh direct sun so deep mineral dyes and cream grounds remain balanced.
- Store wrapped inside clean breathable cotton cloth.

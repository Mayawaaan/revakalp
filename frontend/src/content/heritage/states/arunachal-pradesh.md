## History & Origin

Across the mist-veiled easternmost Himalayas of **Arunachal Pradesh**, communities including the **Apatani, Adi, Galo, Nyishi, and Monpa** cultivated deeply symbolic geometric textiles—where crisp diamonds, stepped mountains, and vivid crimson-and-black stripes signified seasonal renewal, hospitality, and highland pride.

Working alongside master weavers in **Ziro Valley, Aalo (Along), and Pasighat**, contemporary craft initiatives now transform traditional ceremonial *Gale* borders seamlessly onto full-length artisanal cotton and Eri-silk sarees.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Ziro Valley** anchors iconic **Apatani** diamond borders and warm bark-dyed hues; **Along (Aalo) and Pasighat** celebrate bold **Adi and Galo** black-white-scarlet symmetry; and western valleys around **Tawang** infuse Tibetan-influenced cloud and lotus richness.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Sturdy high-density cotton and Eri-silk grounds give Arunachal handlooms crisp body stability—keeping pleated folds clean and dignified while letting richly packed border bands gleam softly along the lower sweep and trailing pallu.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

Drawing directly from traditional **loin-loom** tension craft, weavers group multi-colored extra-weft threads between crisp background picks—forming stepped geometric stars, crisp eye-diamonds, and sharp zigzags with remarkable edge clarity and zero loose threads.

## Cultural Significance

Celebrated across *Dree*, *Solung*, and *Losar* festivities—and worn proudly by modern textile collectors seeking rare eastern Himalayan heritage—Arunachal sarees offer commanding graphic beauty and authentic slow-loom craft.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Gently dry-clean silk-blended or intensely saturated multi-color Arunachal border sarees; pure cottons should be hand-washed gently in cool water separately.
- Dry neatly flat in cool shade without wringing geometric borders.
- Press from reverse using medium heat beneath a soft cotton cloth.
- Store inside clean breathable cotton wraps.

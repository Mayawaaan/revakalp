## History & Origin

In Mizoram's steep eastern ridges, weaving on portable backstrap **loin looms** (*buantawng*) stood historically at the center of community memory—every stripe pattern encoding lineage, ceremony, and mountain identity across heirloom **Puan** wraps.

Over recent decades, master weavers in **Thenzawl and Aizawl** carefully adapted legendary ceremonial cloths—vivid rainbow-banded **Puanchei** and stately black-and-ivory **Ngotekherh**—onto full six-yard sarees prized across India for bold architectural geometry.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Thenzawl** in Serchhip district serves as Mizoram's premier handloom town alongside **Aizawl and Champhai**—producing crisp cotton and silk-blended weaves enriched with traditional motifs like *Kawkpuizik* (young coiled fern shoots), *Lenbuangthuam*, and *Disul* cross-diamonds.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Mizo handloom sarees typically combine crisp, structured combed cotton warps with densely woven extra-weft border panels—delivering taut, confident pleats that highlight stark black-cream contrast or electric *Puanchei* bursts cleanly along the pallu.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

On narrow backstrap loin looms (and coordinated modern frame looms for wider saree yardage), artisans raise fine tensioned warp yarns and work geometric patterns manually using slender bamboo picks—ensuring saturated ruby, emerald, and magenta bands sit crisp and tight without sagging.

## Cultural Significance

Central to *Chapchar Kut* spring bamboo-dance festivals, weddings, and proud cultural evenings nationwide, a Mizo Puan-inspired saree radiates fearless graphic clarity and warm hospitality.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Gently dry-clean brightly colored Puanchei silk/cotton sarees so saturated crimson and charcoal borders stay razor-sharp next to cream fields.
- Avoid warm soaking or chemical bleaches across contrasting monochrome *Ngotekherh* stripes.
- Press gently on reverse while slightly damp with medium heat.
- Store cleanly folded inside dry cotton wraps.

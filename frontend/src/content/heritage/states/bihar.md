## History & Origin

Along the southern banks of the Ganga, **Bhagalpur** and neighboring **Champanagar** have traded reeled and spun wild silk for centuries—earning the moniker "Silk City" for transforming rustic forest tussar cocoons into cool, breathable, champagne-toned drapes.

Just northward across the Mithila plains, **Madhubani and Jitwarpur** women nurtured a celebrated wall and floor painting heritage featuring double-line mineral inkwork, which from the 1960s onward translated exquisitely onto crisp Bhagalpuri tussar saree borders and pallus.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Champanagar and Nathnagar** in Bhagalpur spin *Reeled Tussar*, crisp *Katia*, chunky *Ghicha*, and contemporary *Tussar-Linen* yarns. Meanwhile, **Madhubani and Darbhanga** house family painting ateliers where natural indigo, turmeric, lampblack, and pomegranate dyes trace epic tales across prepared silk.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Unlike slippery satin silks, Bhagalpuri Tussar carries an airy crispness that stays cool against the skin even during warm afternoon events—while handspun *Ghicha* threads provide subtle matte richness across natural fawn, biscuit, and dyed jewel hues.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

Unwinding *Daba* and *Modal* wild cocoons requires patient wet-reeling and hand-twisting along earthen pots (*matka*), yielding textured yarn with organic thickness variations.

On **Madhubani Tussar** sarees, artists first wash and size the silk with gentle starch before dipping bamboo pens wrapped in cotton (*kalam*) into dark outline ink (*kachni* hatching or *bharni* vibrant color fills).

## Cultural Significance

Cherished by educators, art patrons, and festive hosts alike, a Bhagalpuri Tussar or Mithila-painted saree effortlessly balances literary poise with living folk expression.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Always dry-clean Bhagalpuri Tussar and hand-painted Madhubani silk sarees; never scrub painted borders in water.
- Press painted pallu and border zones strictly from the unpainted reverse side through soft cotton.
- Shield mineral-painted silks from harsh midday sun to protect natural pigments.
- Fold loosely inside clean breathable muslin bags.

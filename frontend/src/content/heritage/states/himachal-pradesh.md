## History & Origin

High in the valleys of **Kullu, Mandi, and Kinnaur**, harsh alpine winters demanded warm woven garments, yet mountain weavers transformed practical necessity into joyful geometric poetry—adorning plain ivory, camel, and charcoal drapes (*pattu*) with glowing rainbow bands drawn from monastery textiles and alpine wildflowers.

In modern winter wardrobes, these very **Kulluvi and Kinnauri** extra-weft bands are woven seamlessly along warm silk-wool and soft tussar-wool sarees tailored for misty winter receptions.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Bhuntar and Kullu** cultivate multi-border *Chashme-Bulbul* patterns paired with vivid yellow, magenta, green, and saffron mountain checks, while **Kinnaur** (Reckong Peo and Sangla) preserves dense Buddhist and Hindu symbolic motifs woven in classic five auspicious colors.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Woven across fine silk-merino or warm tussar-wool grounds, Himachal winter sarees balance cozy shoulder insulation with surprisingly agile pleats—keeping the wearer comfortable outdoors without bulky weight.

Plain soothing oatmeal, biscuit, charcoal, or deep maroon bodies let the intricate multi-hued border and pallu ribs gleam vividly.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

On horizontal wooden pit looms fitted with hand-lifted heddles, artisans insert bright *extra-weft* geometric motifs—using tiny hand bobbins rather than long floats—to build raised diamond ladders, stepped mountains, and temple triangles row by row along the border.

## Cultural Significance

During Kullu Dussehra processions, Kinnauri winter harvests, and sunlit hillside weddings across North India, these vibrant mountain borders bring unmistakable Himalayan clarity and warmth.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Always dry-clean silk-wool and fine Kullu-bordered sarees to protect delicate animal fibers and bright dyed extra-weft bands.
- Never soak or agitate in hot water, which causes wool fibers to felt and tighten.
- Air flat indoors away from radiators or harsh sun.
- Store folded cleanly with mild cedarwood inside sealed breathable cotton bags during summer months.

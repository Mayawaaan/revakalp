## History & Origin

Along the Coromandel Coast, Andhra Pradesh's weavers honed an unmatched mastery of high-count cotton muslins and featherlight silk blends—crafted for coastal humidity and patronized historically by coastal estates and the courts of Venkatagiri and Vizianagaram.

Uppada along East Godavari revived classical two-weaver Jamdani artistry onto gossamer silk warps, making sheer shimmering drapes weighing barely a few hundred grams a modern celebration favorite.

## Regional Craft & Weaving Centers

**Uppada** (Kakinada district) crafts lustrous translucent silk *Jamdani* and heavy *Tissue* sarees; **Venkatagiri** (Tirupati district) specializes in famously soft, durable fine cottons framed in solid gold borders; **Mangalagiri** near Guntur weaves clean combed cottons edged with signature *Nizam* zari lines; and **Dharmavaram** weaves stately muted-gold wedding silks.

Adding hand-painted **Srikalahasti** and block-printed **Machilipatnam Kalamkari** turns coastal cotton and tussar into botanical story scrolls.

## Material Characteristics & Drape

Uppada Jamdani feels almost cloud-weight on the shoulder while catching candlelit gold shimmer across every floating floral *butta*.

Mangalagiri and Venkatagiri cottons gain extraordinary softness with age—combining whisper-thin breathability with taut, disciplined borders that pleat cleanly at the waist.

## Craftsmanship & Loom Technique

Traditional **Uppada Jamdani** seats two weavers side by side at the loom: instead of punching mechanical cards, they lift selected warp clusters by hand and wind discontinuous gold or colored silk needles around those threads—leaving an almost invisible weave structure with zero heavy backing floats.

Meanwhile, **Mangalagiri** cottons weave without gaps using dense shuttle beats followed by crisp ribbed gold borders.

## Cultural Significance

Beloved across South India for daytime muhurthams, festive temple visits, and summer celebrations, Andhra Pradesh's weaves effortlessly bridge sheer comfort with refined ceremonial luminance.

## Care & Preservation Guidance

- Dry-clean Uppada Jamdani, Silk-Tissue, and Dharmavaram sarees to protect sheer warps and metallic extra-weft threads.
- Wash fine Venkatagiri or Mangalagiri cottons gently in cool water without harsh bleach or vigorous wringing; keep deep borders shaded while drying.
- Press Uppada silk gently from the back beneath a soft pressing cloth.
- Wrap neatly in unbleached cotton sleeves for seasonal storage.
